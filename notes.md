# ERP Partner Programs Research — Summary Notes

Research compiled: July 3, 2026. Full structured data in [`erp.json`](./erp.json).

## Data Quality by Vendor

**Strong public data (multiple official docs + press):**
- **NetSuite** — official SPP program guide PDF and Oracle press releases gave concrete revenue-share ranges (30–50%/10–30%) and a clear ecosystem count. See the [NetSuite SPP program guide (PDF)](https://www.netsuite.com/portal/assets/pdf/ds-ns-solution-provider-program-2.pdf) and [Oracle's Oct 2025 AI partner program announcement](https://www.oracle.com/sg/news/announcement/sw25-netsuite-expands-partner-program-accelerate-ai-innovation-2025-10-06/).
- **SAP** — the [PartnerEdge Program Guide (PDF)](https://partneredge.sap.com/content/dam/public/dni/pe_program_guide_preview.pdf) disclosed exact annual fees (€3,700/€2,000) and MDF funding percentages, though revenue share itself stayed hidden. [SAP News](https://news.sap.com/2025/01/sap-partneredge-cloud-driven-business-in-ai-era/) provided a reliable 25,000+ partner count and three dated 2025-2026 program updates.
- **Acumatica** — publishes an unusually transparent "$0 fees" policy directly on its [partner blog](https://www.acumatica.com/blog/the-acumatica-ecosystem-a-place-where-partners-can-thrive/), plus a flat 15% referral rate in the [2024 Alliance Program Guide (PDF)](https://www.acumatica.com/media/2024/09/Acumatica-Alliance-Program-Guide-FNL-July-2024.pdf). The [2026 CRN Partner Program Guide](https://www.crn.com/news/channel-news/2026/5-star-software-vendor-programs) supplied recent, dated program changes directly from Acumatica's own submission.
- **Odoo** — surprisingly rich third-party tracking ecosystem (Odoo Unlocked newsletter, Alten Capital, JustDoers directory) gave month-by-month partner counts and a full commission ladder (10%→20% on licenses, up to 50% on Odoo.sh) sourced from an [official Odoo partner training video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYsD1EaE1pA).
- **Microsoft** — no Dynamics-specific numbers, but strong secondary sourcing (Novistra Capital, CRN, Microsoft's own blog) documented the FY26 shift to a $1M CSP revenue bar and new AI-linked incentive boosts.

## Thin or Weak Data Vendors

- **Priority Software** — the [official partner page](https://www.priority-software.com/become-a-partner/) discloses no tiers, no rates, and no certification costs — only vague language ("a rewarding revenue-sharing scheme," "100% services fees retention"). This is the thinnest public partner page of all 10 vendors.
- **Deltek** — the [official Deltek Partner Network page](https://www.deltek.com/en/partners) does not name a "Solutions Partner Program," "CPA Partner Program," or "Advocate Program" as distinct structures (despite these being commonly referenced in secondary sources) — it presents one unified network with only two visible partner types (Solutions Partners, Technology Integration Partners) and a single hard number: 200+ partners/integrations.
- **Infor** — current IPN documentation is scarce; the best available data (Associate/Silver/Gold tiers, 40%/25% Gold commission) comes from 2011–2016 sources ([Diginomica](https://diginomica.com/briefing-review-can-infors-partner-strategy-keep-pace-with-its-digital-pursuits), [CRN](https://www.crn.com/news/channel-programs/230500015/infor-develops-new-program-standard-contracts-for-its-channel-partners)) with no confirmed recent restructuring found.
- **Sage** — no dated 2025-2026 program announcement could be confirmed publicly; revenue share is absent from official pages, with only a third-party-sourced ~30% commission figure for Sage Intacct VARs ([cashflowfrog.com](https://cashflowfrog.com/partners-sage-intacct/)).
- **Epicor** — official page names four partner relationship types (Sell/Service/Refer/Develop) but omits tiers and revenue share; the most concrete numbers (tier revenue thresholds of $605K/$1.045M/$2.09M) come from a third-party MSP blog ([2WTech](https://2wtech.com/understanding-the-epicor-certified-partner-program-levels/)), not Epicor itself.

## Best Sources Found (by type)

- **Official program guide PDFs (highest value):** [NetSuite SPP guide](https://www.netsuite.com/portal/assets/pdf/ds-ns-solution-provider-program-2.pdf), [SAP PartnerEdge guide](https://partneredge.sap.com/content/dam/public/dni/pe_program_guide_preview.pdf), [Acumatica Alliance Program Guide](https://www.acumatica.com/media/2024/09/Acumatica-Alliance-Program-Guide-FNL-July-2024.pdf).
- **Vendor newsroom announcements:** [SAP News](https://news.sap.com/2025/01/sap-partneredge-cloud-driven-business-in-ai-era/), [Oracle/NetSuite press releases](https://www.oracle.com/sg/news/announcement/sw25-netsuite-expands-partner-program-accelerate-ai-innovation-2025-10-06/), [Microsoft's official blog](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2025/03/24/microsoft-at-50-the-journey-and-future-of-the-partner-ecosystem/).
- **Independent channel trade press:** [CRN's 2026 Partner Program Guide](https://www.crn.com/news/channel-news/2026/5-star-software-vendor-programs) was the single best source for dated, vendor-confirmed 2025-2026 program changes across multiple vendors simultaneously.
- **Third-party partner directories:** [ERP Research](https://www.erpresearch.com/) (NetSuite, SAP, Sage partner counts) and [JustDoers/Odoo Unlocked](https://www.justdoers.com/odoo-partners-directory) (Odoo) offered the most reliable independent ecosystem-size estimates where vendors don't publish exact figures.

## Punchy Insights for SEO Copy

1. **Half of the 10 major ERP vendors keep their partner commission rates completely secret.** SAP, Priority Software, Deltek, and Infor (current structure) publish no revenue-share percentage anywhere on their public partner pages — and Epicor's numbers come only from a 2016 trade-press quote, not the vendor itself.
2. **Acumatica is the only ERP vendor that charges partners literally nothing to join** — no signup fee, no annual fee, no certification cost — a deliberate contrast to competitors charging $7,500–$14,000/year, per [Acumatica's own partner blog](https://www.acumatica.com/blog/the-acumatica-ecosystem-a-place-where-partners-can-thrive/).
3. **Odoo's partner network size depends entirely on who's counting:** official event stats cite "10,000+" partners, one newsletter tracker says 11,291, and the vendor's own public directory shows only 3,980 — meaning roughly two-thirds of "partners" are unlisted, uncertified accounts sitting in the entry-level Learning tier.
4. **SAP's partner fees are the most granular and transparent of any vendor researched** — down to the euro (€3,700/year for Sell/Service, €2,000/year for Build-only) — yet SAP discloses zero information about what commission a partner actually earns on a deal.
5. **Microsoft just tripled the bar to be a serious Dynamics reseller:** the Direct Cloud Solution Provider revenue minimum jumped from roughly $300K/year to $1M/year in FY26, according to [Novistra Capital's ecosystem report](https://novistra.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/DX_Microsoft-Ecosystem_Report_Dec-2025_FINAL.pdf) — a filter that will push smaller Dynamics partners toward indirect/distributor models.
6. **Epicor's partner tiers are pure pay-to-play by revenue, not skill:** Platinum status requires exceeding $2.09M in annual Epicor-sourced revenue plus 14 dedicated sales/marketing staff — a bar most SMB-focused implementers will never clear, according to [2WTech's analysis](https://2wtech.com/understanding-the-epicor-certified-partner-program-levels/).
