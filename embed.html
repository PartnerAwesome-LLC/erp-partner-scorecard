Microsoft Dynamics 365 (Business Central + Finance & Operations)

Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program (MAICPP) — Cloud Solution Provider (CSP) model + Solutions Partner for Business Applications designation

Website

https://partner.microsoft.com/partnership/solutions-partner-program

Partner types

Reseller / VAR, SI / Implementation, ISV / Development, Referral

Tiers
  • Cloud Solution Provider (CSP): Indirect Reseller (no revenue minimum) / Direct Bill (requires $1M trailing-12-month revenue in FY26)
  • Solutions Partner designations across 6 solution areas (incl. Business Applications for Dynamics 365/Power Platform), each requiring a Partner Capability Score of 70/100
  • System Integrator (SI) and Managed Service Provider (MSP) tracks with their own incentive programs
  • FY27 additions: Agentic Partner Capability Score; Frontier partner specialization; frontier transformation engineer badge
Program structure note

Microsoft retired the old Gold/Silver competency model in October 2022 in favor of solution-area 'Solutions Partner' designations (e.g., Solutions Partner for Business Applications) earned via performance, skilling, and customer success points rather than a linear tier ladder.

Revenue share range

FY27 (Jul 1, 2026–Jun 30, 2027): Dynamics 365 incentives shift from flat run-rate rebates to premium-SKU Strategic Product Accelerators plus a year-on-year Growth Accelerator; third-party Sherweb analysis states up to 19.5% earning opportunity, while Microsoft does not publish a Dynamics-specific rate table. MSPs can earn 15% Partner Earned Credit on managed Azure consumption.

Deal registration

Yes — Managed via Microsoft co-sell and referral programs; in FY27 Azure IP co-sell is Marketplace-first and PRACR is no longer the broad co-sell mechanism; specific protection period not publicly disclosed

MDF available

Yes — MDF available through the Partner Incentives program, tied to designation level and co-sell performance

Certification cost

Individual Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 exams typically ~$165 USD each; FY27 Business Applications skilling updates replace MB-280 with AB-210, Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations/Supply Chain solution-architect skilling with AB-100, PL-200 with AB-410, and MB-240 with AB-250 for affected designations/specializations.

Annual partner fee

Not a flat fee — gated by revenue thresholds; Direct Bill CSP requires $1M+ trailing-12-month cloud revenue in FY26 (raised from a prior ~$300K/year bar), per Novistra Capital ecosystem report

Ecosystem size

500,000+ partners across the overall Microsoft Cloud Partner Program ecosystem (not Dynamics-specific); more than 1,500 partner businesses hold Microsoft's diverse/minority-owned certification (per CRN)

Geographic reach

Global

Best-fit ICP

SMB (Business Central) to Enterprise (Finance & Operations)

5 of 10 ERPs Don't Publicly Disclose Revenue Share

Half of the ten major ERP vendors researched keep their partner commission rates completely secret. SAP, Priority Software, Deltek, and Infor's current program structure publish no revenue-share percentage anywhere on their public partner pages, and Epicor's only available figures trace back to a 2016 trade-press quote rather than the vendor itself — see SAP's partner program page and 2WTech's Epicor tier analysis. For prospective partners, this means real margin numbers typically only surface after signing an NDA or entering a sales conversation.

Why this matters for partnerships: opacity around revenue share makes it harder for prospective partners to compare programs upfront, favoring vendors willing to publish real numbers.

Acumatica Is the Only ERP Vendor That Charges Partners Literally Nothing to Join

Acumatica markets a deliberate "zero startup or annual fees" policy — no signup fee, no annual fee, no certification cost — explicitly contrasting itself with competitors charging $7,500–$14,000 per year, according to Acumatica's own partner blog. Free training and badges (Sales Consultant, Pre-Sales Engineer, Business Consultant) reinforce a low-friction, volume-focused partner-recruitment strategy rather than a pay-to-play gate.

Why this matters for partnerships: a zero-fee model lowers the barrier for smaller implementers to become certified, but it also means Acumatica's ecosystem quality bar rests entirely on performance requirements, not upfront investment.

Odoo's Partner Count Depends Entirely on Who's Counting

Odoo's reported ecosystem size varies wildly by source and date: official event stats cite "10,000+" partners, the Odoo Unlocked newsletter tracker reported 11,291 as of September 2025, yet the vendor's own public directory shows only 3,980 listed and certified partners as of March 2026 — per Odoo Unlocked #012 and the JustDoers Odoo Partners Directory. That gap implies roughly two-thirds of "partners" are unlisted, uncertified accounts sitting in the entry-level Learning tier.

Why this matters for partnerships: headline partner-count claims can overstate the size of a vendor's active, certified channel by a wide margin — always check the directory, not just the press release.

SAP's Fees Are the Most Transparent on Record — Its Commissions Are the Most Opaque

SAP discloses its partner fees down to the euro — €3,700/year for the Sell/Service track and €2,000/year for Build-only — per the SAP PartnerEdge Program Guide, and even publishes granular MDF co-funding percentages via SAP News. Yet SAP discloses zero information about what commission a partner actually earns on a closed deal, leaving the single most important number for partner economics undisclosed.

Why this matters for partnerships: fee transparency is not the same as margin transparency — partners should ask directly about deal economics rather than assume detailed fee schedules imply comparable commission clarity.
